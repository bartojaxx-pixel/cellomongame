Rules

Battle

  • Your cards are at the bottom. Hover to pop them up. Click to select, then press “Play Selected Card”.
  • Opponent’s cards are always hidden (card backs only).
  • Cards stay until HP = 0. HP does not reset between rounds.
  • Winner stays; loser is replaced by the next chosen card.
  • KO triggers shrink-pop animation.
  • Influenza (4★ Virus): DMG = 0. Special – Infection Swap (swaps current HP with the opponent’s active card).

Boosters

  • Protein → +1 HP or +1 DMG
  • DNA → +2 HP or +2 DMG
  • Nucleus gains both +2 HP and +2 DMG from DNA
  • Boosters attach visually to the card

Shiny / Storage / Shop / Quiz

  • Shiny odds by rarity; potions improve them.
  • Shinies go to Storage; sell for coins.
  • Quiz: correct answer earns a potion.
