Rules

Battle

  • Horizontal battlefield – your card vs AI card.
  • Cards stay until HP reaches 0. HP does not reset between rounds.
  • Winner stays on the field; loser is replaced by the next card from the deck.
  • KO triggers a shrink-pop animation.
  • Battle log scrolls horizontally.
  • Influenza has a special infection ability (steals 1 HP from opponent and heals itself).

Boosters

  • Protein → +1 HP or +1 DMG (player choice).
  • DNA → +2 HP or +2 DMG.
  • Nucleus gains both +2 HP and +2 DMG from DNA.
  • Boosters attach visually to the card.
  • Booster deck size depends on match mode.

Shiny System

  • Odds: 1–2★ → 1/100 · 3★ → 1/200 · 4★ → 1/400 · 5★ → 1/800
  • Potions reduce the denominator (make shinies more likely).
  • Shiny banner animation appears; shinies go automatically to Storage.

Storage & Shop

  • Storage shows all shinies. Sell duplicates for coins (value by rarity).
  • Shop: potions cost 10 coins and improve shiny odds.

Quiz

  • “Which cell type is this organelle from?”
  • Correct → earn a potion. Wrong → show correct answer.

Layout

  • Left: Player deck · Center: Battlefield · Right: Boosters
  • Buttons: Attack, Use Booster, End Match
  • Match modes: 3v3, 5v5, 7v7
